Endpoints

GET /api/v1/presentations
List presentations.
POST /api/v1/presentations
Create a presentation.
GET /api/v1/presentations/{slug}
Read a presentation and its Markdown source.
PATCH /api/v1/presentations/{slug}
Update its title, source, or theme. Slugs are immutable.
DELETE /api/v1/presentations/{slug}
Delete it unless a live session is active.
